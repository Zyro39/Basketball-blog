Basic Basketball Skills

November 3, 2026

Basketball requires several important skills. These include dribbling, shooting, passing, and defense.

Practicing these skills regularly can help players become better and more confident on the court.

How to Improve Your Vertical Jump

November 2, 2026

A strong vertical jump can help players with rebounding, blocking shots, and scoring near the basket.

Exercises like squats, lunges, calf raises, and jump training can help develop leg strength.

Basketball Positions Explained

November 1, 2026

There are five traditional basketball positions:

  • Point Guard - Leads the team's offense.
  • Shooting Guard - Often focuses on scoring.
  • Small Forward - Helps with scoring and defense.
  • Power Forward - Plays near the basket and rebounds.
  • Center - Usually protects the basket and gets rebounds.
